Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This privacy policy covers the iPhone app Zapli (dev.anyapp.preview), distributed by SynapTech s. r. o. (“we”). For any privacy question, write to the feedback email address shown for Zapli in TestFlight.

In short: Zapli does not collect, send or share your data. It stays on your iPhone unless you share it yourself, and we never receive it.

What Zapli does

Zapli installs and runs small tools: the ones that come with it, such as a to-do list, a water log, a blood pressure log, an expense tracker, loyalty cards and three games, and the ones you import: a file you pick in the Files app, or text you paste. It reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste, and installs nothing until you tap Install.

Your data is kept on your iPhone

The tools and everything you enter in them are stored in Zapli’s private storage on your iPhone. That can be sensitive data, such as your blood pressure readings, what you spend or your loyalty card numbers. We never receive any of it. Zapli keeps this data out of your iPhone’s iCloud and computer backups, so it does not move to a new phone. Zapli has no code of its own that connects to the internet, and it contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting code.

Tools with their own code

A tool that contains its own code runs in a web view of its own, with its data kept apart from every other tool. A tool you import runs in WebKit’s Lockdown Mode, where it cannot reach the internet. If you switch an imported tool to Full speed, Zapli warns you first: in that mode it cannot fully stop that tool from reaching the internet, so the tool could send what it stores or what you type into it. Tools that come with Zapli run at full speed; they contain no code that connects to the internet.

Permissions

Zapli uses Face ID or your passcode only to unlock a tool you protect with a lock. iOS tells Zapli only whether unlocking succeeded; Zapli never receives biometric data. Zapli asks for no other permission: no camera, photos, location, contacts or notifications.

When data leaves Zapli

When you choose it: when you copy something from a tool, or share something through iOS’s share sheet from a tool at Full speed. Your data then goes to the app or service you chose, under its own terms; we are not involved and receive nothing.

TestFlight and Apple

Zapli reaches you through Apple’s TestFlight, and Apple processes data under its own privacy policy. We see in App Store Connect the testers’ names and email addresses, and the usage and crash information Apple provides to developers, with any screenshots and comments you send as feedback. We use them only to run the test and fix problems.

Retention and deletion

Data in Zapli stays on your iPhone until you delete it: deleting a tool in Zapli deletes its data, and deleting Zapli from your iPhone deletes everything in its private storage. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete. We remove your email address from the testers list when the test ends or when you ask us.

Security

The data is kept in Zapli’s private storage, protected by iOS’s app sandbox; other apps on your iPhone cannot read it. The tools in Zapli are kept apart from each other, and none of them can use your iPhone’s features through Zapli.

Children

Zapli is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, Zapli shows the new version here, and we publish it on our policy’s web page, each with a new date.
